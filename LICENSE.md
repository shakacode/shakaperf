activation, no telemetry, no lockout. Nothing phones home.

  takes a minute. The software never checks it.

  After your first year, your organization needs a paid plan (today that is

  fewer than 10 people work for your organization (counting all
  affiliates)? Was its revenue in the last twelve months under US $1M? Has
  it raised under US $1M in outside capital, ever?* Charities, educational
  institutions, and hospitals stay free at any size. Personal projects,
  education, and public open source projects stay free too.
- **Do not resell it.** You may not sell ShakaPerf, publish a rebranded or
  competing fork, or run it as a hosted service for others. Forking to
